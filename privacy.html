👋Who we are

Play Burst: Group Party Games ("Play Burst", "the app") is made by Sunil Kumar, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the information described here. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. If you have any questions about it, email sunil588.k@gmail.com.

📱What stays on your device

To make the games work, the app saves a few things on your device:

  • the names you enter for your players
  • your own word lists and Truth or Dare packs
  • personal bests, your daily challenge streak and a Game Night in progress
  • your settings, such as sound, theme and appearance

We never receive this information, and the app never sends it anywhere. It is kept until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Like other app data, it may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud or a computer), which are under your control and covered by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

🚫No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, shows no ads and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code from us or anyone else. It does not need the internet to play. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

📷Camera

In Housie, you can choose to scan a ticket code with the camera. The image is read on your device only to find the code; it is not saved, and it is not sent to us or anyone else. The camera is used for nothing else. You can always type the code instead, and you can turn camera access off at any time in the Settings app.

📳Motion

Some games react to how you move the phone: tilting it in Charades and Headbands, and shaking it to roll in Dice Roller and Cowrie Toss. The motion is read on your device only while you play, to tell a tilt or shake; it is not saved, and it is not sent to us or anyone else. Each of these can be turned off in the game, and you can use the on-screen buttons instead.

📤Sharing

Some games let you share a result, and in Housie the host can share ticket codes, ticket pictures or a PDF of tickets. Sharing opens your device's own share sheet; nothing is sent unless you choose an app or person to send it to, and what happens next is up to that app or person.

🍎Information from Apple

Apple runs the App Store and may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example when you download the app or leave a rating. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us crash reports and statistics about how the app performs. Apple shares these without your name or contact details, and you can turn this off at any time with Share With App Developers in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

✉️When you email us

"Suggest a Game or Feature" and "Report a Problem" open an email in your own mail app, with your app version, iOS version and device model filled in. You can edit or remove any of it before sending. If you send it, we receive your email address, those details and whatever you write.

We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems. You provide it voluntarily for that purpose (under the GDPR, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you). Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail), so it may be stored on servers outside your country. We delete support emails once they are no longer needed, and at the latest two years after our last reply. We never sell them or use them for advertising.

🛡️Your rights

Because the app collects no personal data, there is nothing about you held by us, except emails you choose to send. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the DPDP Act in India or US state laws), you may have the right to ask for a copy of that information, to correct it or to have it deleted. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time, and within any period the law requires. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

🧒Age 13 and over

Play Burst is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app itself collects no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 has emailed us, contact us and we will delete that email.

🔄Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting personal data, we will update this policy before it does.
