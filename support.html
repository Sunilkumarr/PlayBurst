Common questions

How do I choose who's playing?

Tap Players at the top of the Home screen. Add everyone once and every game knows who's playing. You can also add guests for a single game.

How do I learn a game's rules?

Every game has a short How to Play guide. It opens by itself the first time you play; after that, tap the ? button at the top of the game.

Do I need the internet?

No. Every game works offline, so it's perfect for road trips and flights.

How do I change the language?

Play Burst follows your device's language. To choose a different one just for Play Burst, open the Settings app, find Play Burst and tap Language. (This option appears once your device has more than one language in Settings → General → Language & Region.)

The app is in English, Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, Indonesian and Turkish.

How does the daily challenge work?

Each day, the Home screen shows one quick solo game with a target to beat. Hit it to keep your streak going. Once it's done, the card shrinks to show your streak.

Prefer not to see it? Turn off Daily Challenge in Settings; your streak is kept.

What is Game Night?

Game Night keeps one scoreboard across several games. Start one from the Home screen, play any games you like, and each game's winner gets a point. End the night to crown a champion.

Can I add my own words or dares?

Yes. Make your own word lists and Truth or Dare packs, and use them in the games that have packs. They stay on your device.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Tap the speaker button on the Home screen to mute everything, or open Settings in the app to turn sound effects, background music and haptics on or off separately.

Why does Housie ask for the camera?

Only to scan ticket codes, which is quicker than typing them. You can always type a code instead, and nothing the camera sees is saved. See the Privacy Policy.

Where are my players, lists and scores stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes them from your device, though they may remain in your own iCloud or computer backups. See the Privacy Policy.
